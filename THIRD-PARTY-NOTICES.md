# Third-Party Notices

Network Optimizer includes data from the projects below.

## Data

### GeoNames

Traceroute hop locations use CLLI place codes derived from GeoNames place names
(`src/NetworkOptimizer.Core/Data/router-places.tsv.gz`, built by `scripts/data/build-router-places.py`).

GeoNames data by [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The place names are
reduced to four-letter city codes, one town per code.

### OurAirports

Traceroute hop locations use airport codes and locations from [OurAirports](https://ourairports.com/data/),
released into the public domain.
